    How to pair

    1. Turn on the thermal printer and keep it close to this device.
    2. Press Pair New Printer.
    3. Pick your printer in the list the browser shows, then press Pair.
    4. Press Test Print to check it works.

    Works in Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac and Android, with Bluetooth (BLE) thermal printers. After opening a new page, the first print asks you to pick the printer again.
